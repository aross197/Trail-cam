AREA 51

TEAM CAMERA SETUP
STANDARD TEAM CAMERA

XTU SV-TCQSW

Solar Trail Camera • 32MP / 2.7K • WiFi + Bluetooth

Bob Mark Rosco

All three team members use this model.

1. Unbox & Power

1
Open the housing. Confirm the pre-installed 32GB microSD is seated.
2
(Optional) Insert 4 × AA alkaline batteries (1.5V only — no rechargeables) for backup.
3
Close housing firmly. Slide switch to TEST. Screen should light up.
If the screen stays dark, charge the internal battery with the USB-C cable for a few hours first.

2. Connect to TrailCam Go App

1
Download TrailCam Go from the App Store or Google Play.
2
Turn on phone Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. Stay close to the camera (under ~15–20 ft).
3
Open the app → tap + → choose Wi-Fi Camera.
4
Select the camera when it appears (usually TrailCam_XXXX). Tap Join when your phone asks to connect to its temporary Wi-Fi.
The camera creates its own Wi-Fi hotspot. It does not join your home network. Range is ~49 ft officially.

3. After Connection

  • Set date & time
  • Choose Photo / Video / Photo+Video mode
  • Set resolution (32MP photo / 2.7K video recommended)
  • Adjust PIR sensitivity (Medium is a good start)
  • Format the SD card in the app or camera menu
When finished, switch from TEST to ON. The screen turns off and the camera enters motion-detection mode.

4. Field Tips

  • Mount 0.5–2 m high, angled slightly down
  • Face north or south (avoid east/west sun glare)
  • Give the solar panel clear sky exposure
  • Clear lens and PIR sensor of debris regularly
